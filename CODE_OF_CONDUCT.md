# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
Critique changes and evidence. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to follow these rules. Maintainers
may remove abusive content or restrict participation. Apply moderation
consistently and keep private reports confidential.

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@kiff.dev](mailto:hello@kiff.dev).
For vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
